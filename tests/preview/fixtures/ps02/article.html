City chase fifth straight home win

Manchester City host Fulham on 21 November with Pep Guardiola's side seeking a fifth successive home victory. Erling Haaland has nine league goals this season, and Rayan Cherki has contributed six in a supporting role.

Fulham arrive with two wins in their last five league matches. Josh King has started every league game and earned a first senior England call-up on 6 November.

City are expected to be without one midfielder through suspension, though the club has not published a squad list. Fulham report no fresh injuries beyond their long-term absentees.
